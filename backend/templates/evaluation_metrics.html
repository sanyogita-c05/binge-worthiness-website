Interpretation:

MAE (Mean Absolute Error): Average difference between actual and predicted binge score. Lower is better.

RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error): Measures prediction error with extra penalty on large mistakes. Lower is better.

R² Score: Shows how well the model explains binge score variation. Closer to 1 is better.
